Important Spiritual Hygiene Notice

Every authentic Rudraksha bead, Siddh Mala, and Kavach dispatched by Gyan Mala Rudraksh undergoes specialized Vedic Pran Pratishtha (consecration and energization) in your name and Gotra prior to shipment. To maintain spiritual purity and hygiene, items that have been physically worn, used in puja rituals, or had their tamper-evident security seal removed cannot be accepted for routine return. Please read our fair guidelines below.

1. 7-Day Return & Replacement Window

We offer a 7-Day Replacement or Return Window from the date your shipment is marked delivered by our courier partners (Blue Dart, Delhivery, DTDC, India Post), under the following circumstances:

  • Transit Damage: The Rudraksha bead, mala thread, silver/gold capping, or laboratory certificate is physically broken, cracked, or deformed during shipping.
  • Wrong Item Received: The product delivered differs materially from what was ordered (e.g., received a 5 Mukhi bead instead of a 7 Mukhi bead, or Java bead instead of Nepal bead).
  • Defective Capping: Defect or damage in the silver or gold wire capping work upon arrival.
  • Missing Authenticity Certificate: If a certified bead is delivered without its physical Government/ISO registered X-ray authenticity certificate.

2. Non-Returnable & Non-Refundable Conditions

Return or refund requests cannot be accepted in the following situations:

  • Requests initiated after 7 calendar days of verified courier delivery.
  • Products whose tamper-evident seal, security tags, or official certification barcode cards have been detached or tampered with.
  • Products that show clear physical signs of wear, immersion in water/oil/perfumes, ritual application of sindoor/sandalwood, or physical drops causing bead fracturing after receipt.
  • Custom personalized astrology consultations, horoscope Kundli chart preparation services, or Vedic Hawan ritual fee (services already performed are irreversible).
  • Minor natural botanical variations: Every Rudraksha is an organic seed of nature (Elaeocarpus ganitrus). Natural variations in shade, curvature, weight (±10%), or surface grain structure are inherent to genuine Himalayan seeds and are not defects.

3. Mandatory Requirement for Transit Damage Claims (Unboxing Video)

Because authentic Rudrakshas and energized precious malas are high-value sacred assets insured with logistic couriers:

  • We request all customers to record a continuous 360-degree unboxing video from the moment the outer tamper-proof courier flyer bag is opened until the product and certificate are inspected.
  • In the rare event of transit damage or missing items, this video serves as indisputable evidence for filing courier insurance claims and ensures immediate, no-questions-asked replacement within 24 hours.

4. Step-by-Step Return & Replacement Process

If you need to exchange or return an eligible product, follow these simple steps:

  1. Notify Our Help Desk: WhatsApp us at +91 91982 54020 or email support@gyanmalarudraksh.com within 7 days of receiving the parcel.
  2. Share Details: Mention your Order ID, recipient phone number, and attach 2–3 clear photographs and/or unboxing video showing the issue.
  3. Return Approval: Our team reviews and responds within 4 to 12 working hours with a Return Authorization.
  4. Reverse Pickup: We arrange a complimentary doorstep courier reverse pickup across most serviceable pin-codes in India. If reverse pickup is unavailable in your remote village/area, our team assists you with trusted Speed Post dispatch and reimburses postal costs.
  5. Verification & Dispatch: Upon receiving the item back at our Mumbai center, we inspect the seal/seed integrity and dispatch a freshly energized replacement or initiate your refund immediately.

5. Refund Mode & Timelines

Once a return is received and verified at our Mumbai hub:

  • Prepaid Online Orders (UPI / Debit / Credit Cards / Net Banking): Refunds are processed back to the original bank account or source card within 5 to 7 business days via our RBI-compliant payment gateway (Razorpay / Cashfree / Bank IMPS).
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders: Refunds for COD orders are transferred directly to your verified Indian Bank Account via NEFT/IMPS or UPI ID. Our billing team will securely request your IFSC code and account number after approval.
  • Store Credit / Vedic Exchange: Customers may also opt for 100% store credit or exchange for any higher or alternative Mukhi bead of their choice with immediate balance adjustment.

6. Order Cancellation Policy

Orders can be cancelled free of charge at any time before the Vedic consecration and courier packaging phase begins (typically within 4 to 6 hours of order placement).

Once an order is handed over to Blue Dart / Delhivery courier partner and a Tracking AWB number is generated, it cannot be recalled mid-transit. In such cases, customers may simply refuse delivery when the courier arrives, and refund will be processed upon transit return.
