Privacy Policy

Our solemn pledge to respect, protect, and safeguard the personal and spiritual data of our patrons worldwide

Privacy Standard: Indian IT Act, 2000 & Global Best Practices • Estd. 2014

At Gyan Mala Rudraksh (Established 2014, Mumbai, India), we hold the sacred trust of our patrons in the highest esteem. This Privacy Policy details our transparent practices regarding the collection, handling, storage, and protection of your personal information when you browse our website, purchase sacred beads, or seek Vedic guidance.

Zero Data Selling Guarantee: We strictly never sell, lease, trade, or rent your personal contact information or astrological birth chart data to any third-party advertisers or marketing agencies under any circumstances.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to fulfill your orders and provide precise spiritual advisory:

  • Contact Information: Your full name, delivery address, postal code, contact phone number, and email address.
  • Consultation & Astrological Details: Date of birth, time of birth, place of birth, and personal concerns submitted voluntarily for Vedic Kundali analysis.
  • Transactional Records: Order identification, purchased products, payment confirmation status, and courier tracking history.

2. Sacred Protection of Astrological & Birth Details

Astrological details provided for personalized recommendation are treated as sacred and confidential spiritual records. They are accessed strictly by our panel of certified Vedic astrologers to perform birth-chart calculations and are never shared outside our internal advisory team.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is utilized strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Dispatching your energized Rudraksha beads and malas to your accurate physical address.
  • Sending real-time order confirmation, Pran-Pratishtha completion notices, and courier tracking links via SMS/WhatsApp.
  • Responding promptly to your support tickets, bead verification requests, and customer care inquiries.
  • Maintaining compliant financial and tax accounting records under applicable Indian statutory laws.

4. Payment Security & Encryption

4.1. All digital transactions are processed through RBI-authorized, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant secure payment gateways.

4.2. Gyan Mala Rudraksh never stores or has access to your sensitive banking credentials, credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or UPI PINs.

5. Trusted Courier Partners

To safely deliver your sacred beads across India and internationally, your shipping name, address, and mobile number are shared solely with reputed logistics partners (such as BlueDart, DTDC, and India Post Speed Post). These partners are legally prohibited from utilizing your details for any purpose other than executing delivery.

6. Cookies & Site Functionality

Our website utilizes essential session cookies solely to preserve your selected currency (INR, USD, GBP), cart items, and wishlist preferences. You may configure your web browser to disable cookies, although this may affect personalized cart features.

7. Your Data Protection Rights

As a patron of Gyan Mala Rudraksh, you have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information stored in your customer account.
  • Request the correction or update of any inaccurate personal details.
  • Request the permanent deletion of your consultation notes from our database at any time.
